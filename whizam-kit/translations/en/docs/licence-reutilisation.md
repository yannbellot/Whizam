## Use and adapt the kit

The MIT License permits use, copying, modification and redistribution of the kit, including for commercial purposes, subject to the conditions in `LICENSE`.

You can build your project on the kit and adapt its sources. Decide explicitly how to license and distribute your own creations, taking reused code and resources into account.

## Preserve notices

The copyright and MIT permission notices must accompany copies or substantial portions of the software. When handing over the kit or a project reusing its code, retain the applicable text, for example in the `LICENSE` file, together with notices for reused elements.

Creating a GitHub repository, making it public or private, or renaming the folder does not replace these conditions.

## Third-party resources and dependencies

Dependencies, fonts and other third-party resources retain their own licenses and notices. The kit's MIT License does not replace them. The documentation font Inter includes its OFL notice in `public/documentation/OFL.txt`.

Before redistribution, identify the elements actually included and retain their applicable notices. This page is neither an exhaustive dependency audit nor a grant of rights to content provided by someone else.

## Hand over the project

A GitHub repository or folder copy can support handover of sources and documentation. Keep applicable licenses and notices in the transferred version; provide access and any required data separately.

See [Sharing your project](guide-whizam/partager-projet.md) to prepare team handover, and the [distribution notice](distribution/README.md) for precautions when copying the kit.
