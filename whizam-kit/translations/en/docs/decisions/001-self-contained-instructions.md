# 001 — Instructions understandable from the kit alone

**24 October 2026 — user requirement clarified, GOV-03 correction.** This decision clarifies [expected product autonomy](../product/starter-kit.md#autonomie-attendue).

## Observation and decision

Instructions can have no outgoing links yet assume knowledge of a manufacturing organization absent from distribution. Such knowledge must not be required to use, understand or evolve the kit.

AGENTS.md, roles, skills, contracts and usage documents define product responsibilities from its root alone. They do not describe manufacturing-specific instructions/consumers. Required resumption procedures/references remain internal; external technical prerequisites are explicitly stated in the README.

Actual tool permissions and user mandate apply everywhere. Autonomy grants no access, publication or neutralization of applicable instructions.

## History and verification

Preserve historical reports with versions/limits, without rewriting observations or using them as current instructions. Evidence absent from distribution is identified as such, not required for usage. Product name and UI Lab demonstration remain unchanged.

The [GOV-03 work item](../work-items/gov-03-self-contained-instructions.md) carries checks/limits: delivered-text review, internal references and structural validation. Documentary corrections imply no new native effectiveness or runtime/data campaign.
