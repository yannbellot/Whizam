## Consequences and limits

Selection stays manual and depends on client/account availability. Policy changes neither mandate, phases, stop rules, reviews, acceptance, permissions, authorized costs nor publication. It activates no provider, subagent or Ultra mode. Other assistants retain adapters without implicit equivalence; site/tests/Storybook stay independent of AI accounts.

Inspection examples describe the delivered starter and must be reassessed for real derived projects. Availability is dated and distinguishes documentation, advertised capabilities and unobserved actual selection. Files imply neither native trials nor benchmarks. Tracking templates remain uninitialized until a real project mandate.
