# Standalone kit distribution

Whizam Kit is used from the root of the received folder after copying, renaming or moving it. This page explains what is delivered, which checks are needed and the edition’s limits. Technical paths and identifiers remain stable; no maintenance parent, external Whizam service or macOS launcher is needed.

## Edition and provenance

The current edition provides the automated three-category design system, Storybook for consultation and Zensical with active FR/EN guides. The application and Storybook content selections are initially empty; `/projects` and UI Lab are not mounted. The [scope](../product/starter-kit.md) and [technical guide](../../TECHNICAL-GUIDE.md) describe current usage.

Provenance `e81cabc78cd6c9d4263961dc03511dbec0e14ac1` and DS-AUTO-01/DIST-01 campaigns identify dated heritage. They do not represent all subsequent changes. `sources.json` describes supplied content through hashes, modes and link targets; it certifies neither behavior nor acceptance. If this copy has its own Git repository, its complete version can be identified locally; a fresh distribution does not transfer the maintainer's `.git/`, and basic web startup does not require Git.

## Transferred contents

Preserve required hidden files and relative skill links. Exclude installations, caches, builds, data, private configurations, accounts and journals under [handoff](../handoff.md). Dependencies are installed from the npm lockfile; sources transfer no Docker volume or system settings. The portal uses Docker without host Python; see the [documentation guide](../documentation.md).

ROADMAP and project-state templates await initialization for a real derived project. Preserved historical documents, including previous technical-reference versions, are classified outside the portal and search. They are not active instructions.

## Checks and limitations

[Compatibility](../compatibility.md) distinguishes declared requirements, tested versions, native trials and human acceptance. The [DS-AUTO-01 report](ds-auto-01.md) and [initial DIST-01 qualification](qualification.md) remain historical evidence; their presence alone qualifies no new edition.

Recipients use their own tools and access. Adapters do not guarantee native discovery by every assistant. Existing projects are not automatically migrated; the former editor and its journals must not be combined with new authorities.

The kit is distributed under the [MIT License](../licence-reutilisation.md), subject to third-party resources’ own licenses. This license establishes neither human acceptance, publication nor production readiness. Any distribution advertised as bilingual requires a translation coverage check separate from its build. Every new qualification identifies its sources, checks actually performed and limitations.
