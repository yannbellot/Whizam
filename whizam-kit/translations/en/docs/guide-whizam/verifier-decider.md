# Checking and deciding

At review, the assistant presents verified access points and delivered scope. Examine agreed pages and journeys in the application, relevant components in Storybook and documented limitations.

## Choose coverage

| Mode | Your participation |
| --- | --- |
| Manual | Follow a review checklist and report observations. |
| Automated — E2E | The assistant runs authorized scenarios and states their coverage. |
| Combined | Automated scenarios are supplemented by your reading and trials. |

Mandatory technical checks apply in every mode. A successful automated scenario proves neither visual satisfaction nor every possible case.

## Give precise feedback

State the page, initial state, actions, observed result and expected result. Use fictional data and hide private information in shared references.

> “On Contact, after submitting an incomplete form, I cannot tell which field to correct. I expect a message near that field and a way to find it.”

The assistant should distinguish reproduced behavior, remaining uncertainty and proposed corrections. You can report comprehension difficulties or visual concerns without diagnosing a technical cause.

## Decide on acceptance

Check results, your review feedback and your acceptance are distinct. If you have not reviewed the result, human review remains pending. A correction may require a targeted retest.

Use [Current state](../project-state.md) and the [Roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) to resume review. The [review and decision rules](../agents/user-decisions.md) define necessary agreements.
