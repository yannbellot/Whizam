# Scoping a first version

Scoping selects a version small enough to implement and review. Prepare the objective, audience, essential pages or journeys, available text and media, and known constraints. A Figma reference can help but is optional.

## A short brief example

> Fictional example: “Atelier presents a business and lets visitors request an appointment. The first version includes an introduction page and a form. I provide texts and a visual reference. Automatic booking and user accounts are out of scope.”

Specify how the form should behave: simulation, storage or actual sending. External services and credentials must not be inferred from this example. If this decision is missing, the assistant must clarify it before implementing the dependent part.

## Review the proposal

Check:

- Who uses the project, and for what task?
- Which screens, content and states will actually be visible?
- What works, and what remains simulated?
- Which features are excluded?
- Which criteria will establish success?

A useful criterion describes an observation: “After valid input, visitors see a confirmation and know what to expect.” Specify whether any data is actually stored or sent.

Ask the assistant to identify missing content rather than inventing your identity, work or services. Fictional verification data must remain recognizable and isolated.

After agreement on scope and verification, the assistant implements authorized work. Read [Checking and deciding](verifier-decider.md) to prepare the review and the [startup workflow](../product/project-start.md) for applicable rules.
