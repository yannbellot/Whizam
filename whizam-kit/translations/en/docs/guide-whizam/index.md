# Discover Whizam

Whizam helps UX/UI and Product Designers design, build and hand off a web application with an assistant that can work in its files. The kit includes an application foundation, components, methods and verification tools. Your application takes its name and objectives from your brief.

You provide intent, content, references and design decisions. The assistant examines the existing project, proposes a slice of work, implements it within the agreed mandate and explains how to review the result. You decide whether to accept a delivery.

## Choose your starting point

- Receiving the kit? Start with [Preparing your kit](preparer-kit.md), then [Creating your first project](premier-projet.md).
- Already have a project? Read [Resuming and handing off](reprendre-transmettre.md).
- Improving a version? Read [Requesting a change](demander-evolution.md).

## Find your way around

| Space | What you review |
| --- | --- |
| Application | Pages and journeys actually implemented for your project. |
| Storybook | Project components, documented variants and states. |
| Documentation | This guide, project decisions and progress, and technical references. |

Initially, the application and Storybook have no business content. The portal contains guides and progress templates: this is expected. The assistant presents verified access points at review time; addresses depend on local services.

In the portal, **Designer documentation** brings together project content and progress; **Technical documentation** explains how the project works. This guide explains how to work with Whizam.

To begin: “Help me prepare the kit and explain what I should provide for my first project.” See the [frequently asked questions](questions-frequentes.md).
