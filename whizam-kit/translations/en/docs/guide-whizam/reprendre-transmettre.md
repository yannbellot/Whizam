# Resuming and handing off

Before resuming, locate your project folder and name. A new conversation should rely on tracking documents and preserve existing work.

> “Read this project’s current state and roadmap, then identify the next step. Do not reinitialize the project.”

## Find progress

Under Designer documentation → Project progress, consult [Current state](../project-state.md), the [Roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) and defined project stages. Current state supports resumption; the roadmap tracks milestones and statuses; stage folders collect preparation, feedback and decisions. This guide does not replace those documents.

In a new kit, templates remain uninitialized and no business stage is defined. Their presence does not authorize historical kit work.

## Stop or restart

> “Check this project’s services and propose how to restart them. Preserve data and other projects.”

The assistant identifies relevant services and explains accessible operations. A port or folder name alone cannot identify a service. If manual intervention is needed, the assistant must explain its purpose and where to perform it.

## Prepare a handoff

Request a handoff covering delivered scope, temporary content, completed checks, limits and next action. Developers should be able to find useful sources and documents without your conversation.

Moving an existing instance requires preserving configurations and data. Distributing a new kit excludes accounts, caches and private configuration; Docker data requires separate backup. Ask the assistant to identify the case before copying.

Full procedures are in [handoff](../handoff.md), [operations](../operations.md) and [backup](../backup-and-restore.md).
