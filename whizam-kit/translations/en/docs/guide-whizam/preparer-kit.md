# Preparing your kit

Work on a copy of the kit to preserve a reusable template. Choose a project location and keep its files together, including required hidden files and internal links. Open this folder as the root in your development assistant.

## Before you start

Prepare your intent or brief if available, or request a demonstration. No assistant selection is requested: each tool follows its entry point. Adapters have their own compatibility limits; their presence does not prove testing in your environment.

The assistant must check the folder and prerequisites before installing anything. Some operations may require your intervention or tool permission. It should explain what is missing and how that affects the next step.

> “I work with Codex. Check the prerequisites in this copy of the kit and prepare a proposal to start my project.”

Expect a clear account of the target folder, available tools, missing prerequisites and proposed preparation. Preserve any previously used folder; do not request a reset to resolve a startup problem.

After approval, the assistant can prepare the environment within the agreed scope. Technical preparation does not invent project features.
