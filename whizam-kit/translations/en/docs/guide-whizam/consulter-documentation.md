# Reading the documentation

**The documentation preserves information needed to understand, evolve and hand over your project.** It brings together content, design choices, technical decisions and work tracking.

The assistant expands it as work progresses. You can consult it to check progress, find a decision or prepare the next steps.

## Open the documentation

In your conversation with the assistant, ask:

> Open my project's documentation.

The assistant checks access to the portal and starts it if needed. It tells you about any missing prerequisites.

**The documentation opens in your browser.** Its address depends on your local environment: use the link verified by the assistant to access the correct project.

## Find your way around the portal

The portal is organized into three sections, accessible from the navigation.

### User guide

**The guide explains how to work with Whizam.** It covers getting started, using the tools, sharing the project and customizing the kit.

### Project progress

**Project progress brings together the current state, roadmap and work stages.**

You can find completed work, pending decisions, checks and next actions.

In a fresh kit, the tracking templates await the start of your project. They are filled in progressively with your objectives and completed work.

### Technical documentation

**Technical documentation describes how the project works and the conditions needed to operate it.**

It covers architecture, data, installation, security and development tools. It serves as a reference for the assistant and helps a technical team take over.

You can ask the assistant to explain a document or the consequences of a technical choice.

## Track project progress

In **Project progress**, three reference points explain where work stands:

- **Current state** describes completed work, pending decisions and the next action.
- **The roadmap** presents objectives, work items and their progress.
- **Project stages** group preparation, verification and decision documents associated with the work.

**A technically verified result may still await your acceptance.** Tracking distinguishes these moments to avoid presenting work awaiting review as finished.

## Find information

Use the navigation to browse sections or **the portal search** to find a term, component or decision.

You can also ask the assistant directly:

> Where did we document how registration works?

> Which decisions remain before the next version?

> Summarize the changes made since our last session.

The assistant reads the project documents to answer and points you to useful sources.

## Keep documentation current

**Project changes must be accompanied by updates to the relevant documents.** Although Whizam updates its documentation automatically, you can ask the assistant to check its consistency:

> Check that the documentation matches the current project version and flag missing or outdated information.

If you find an error, identify the page and desired correction. The assistant can edit the corresponding source file.

Documentation is part of your project folder. **It accompanies handover** and helps another person understand completed work, its limits and the next steps.
