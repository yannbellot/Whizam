# analyze-design-references

Inputs: accessible references, product context, version/screenshot and formats. Read docs/agents/skill-contract.md; use only tools actually available.

## Work and output

Record composition, hierarchy, represented variants/states and observable intent. Distinguish visible elements, inferences and missing information; a static screen proves neither interaction nor a business rule. Compare references without silently treating the newest as authoritative.

Produce a usable synthesis for the design system/journeys, recording the source and limits of each observation. Do not turn all screenshot values into approved tokens. For inaccessible references, record the limitation and request only essential material; continue independent analysis. Identify reversible proposals for review; resolve conflicts under AGENTS. No implicit implementation or Figma import.
