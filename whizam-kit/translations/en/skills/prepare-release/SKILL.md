# prepare-release

Inputs: candidate version, delivered scope, criteria and applicable reports. Read docs/agents/skill-contract.md and project requirements.

## Work and output

Distinguish a demonstrable prototype, transferable MVP and exposed pilot. Link each criterion to evidence of the correct version; check installation, data, journeys, builds, reviews and documentation, including unperformed human checks. Avoid unrelated repeated campaigns without reason, but requalify invalidated checks.

Prepare a proposed version/tag dossier, result matrix, limits and useful decisions. A missing mandatory check or blocking defect prevents a full-delivery claim. Verify licenses/confidentiality/attributions before sharing; proposing a license does not adopt it. Commits/local integration follow AGENTS; push, Actions, releases, hosting and deployment need separate authorization. Preparation can proceed without these authorizations.
